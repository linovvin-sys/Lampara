Pick where you stand (A) and where you are going (B). The route follows the walkways the admin drew in Campus Paths. Each engine draws the same chevron path, so you can see which one stays locked to the ground. Nothing here changes the main Lampara pages or database.

2. Pick an engine and walk the route

Camera, GPS and compass need HTTPS. Over plain http://localhost the pages open but the sensors are blocked on a phone. Use your ngrok URL.

How to test fairly

  1. Stand outside with open sky. Indoors, GPS and compass are unreliable and every engine will look like it drifts. Wait until GPS shows ±10 m or better (tap the i button for the numbers).
  2. Set A to your position and B to a building entrance or a spot on a walkway (or capture your own location), 40–80 m away. Optionally add C: at B the arrow switches to C.
  3. Open an engine, tap Start, and hold the phone upright as you would when walking.
  4. Check: does the first chevron sit on the ground in front of you, and does the line point along the real walkway?
  5. Walk toward B for 20 m. Do the chevrons stay on the ground and slide past you at walking pace, or do they swim?
  6. Turn 360 degrees slowly. Do the chevrons stay put in the world?
  7. Tap Finish & rate and record what you saw. Repeat each engine from the same spot.
